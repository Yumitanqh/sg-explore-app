## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end travel editorial elegance, balancing Singapore’s lush garden-city greenery with vibrant modern gastronomy and nightlife. The visual tone is authoritative yet sun-drenched and welcoming, tailored to discerning travelers, culture seekers, and urban explorers. 

The aesthetic synthesizes:
- **Refined Editorial Distinction:** Large-scale transitional serifs deliver the gravitas of luxury heritage journalism and travel periodicals.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Restrained geometric layouts, crisp framing lines, and warm ivory canvases prevent tropical vibrancy from feeling chaotic.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Organic undertones, botanical depths, and warm terracotta accents conjure sunlit promenades, spice heritage, and canopy-shaded streets.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic luxury tone rooted in botanical depth and sun-warmed natural textures.

- **Primary (`#0F382A`):** Deep botanical emerald green. Anchors top-level headers, primary brand actions, active state markers, and structural grounding elements.
- **Secondary (`#F26440`):** Warm tropical coral/terracotta. Applied intentionally to key call-to-actions, promotional badges, vibrant category tags, and video play triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#F5A623`):** Golden spice saffron. Reserved for rating stars, spotlight ribbons, transit highlights, and culinary accents.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm ivory sand background providing an organic, non-glare foundation that softens contrast.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces layered against ivory canvases to yield subtle natural depth.
- **Text Charcoal (`#1B2421`):** Deep botanical charcoal for high-legibility body prose and headings.
- **Text Muted Sage (`#5A6C64`):** Subdued green-slate for meta-information, secondary labels, transit codes, and microcopy.
- **Subtle Hairline Borders (`#E8E4DC`):** Warm structural dividers providing crisp, low-contrast definition without heavy drop shadows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear intentional contrast:
- **Playfair Display:** Exclusive to editorial headings, discovery titles, article leads, and hero declarations. Imparts timeless heritage and literary prestige.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Assigned to UI elements, body copy, itinerary steps, tags, ratings, map overlays, and operational data. Its geometric cleanliness balances the high-contrast flourishes of Playfair Display.
- **Microcopy & Metadata:** Displayed in uppercase or semi-bold tracking for rapid scanning across badges, MRT station indicators, and metadata pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous editorial white space, structured through a fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens and a 4-column grid on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1200px):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas constrained to `1320px` to maintain optimal line length and visual composure.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Cards reflow into 2-column editorial configurations.
- **Mobile (up to 767px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontal scrolling lists are utilized for category filter pills and media previews to maximize vertical efficiency.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card padding is consistently anchored to `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`). Section separators utilize `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) or double `space-xl` (`5rem`) to delineate major discovery narratives.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy favors clean framing over heavy drop shadows to sustain an editorial aesthetic:

- **Primary Architecture (Low-Contrast Outlines):** Cards and elevated components sit upon the warm ivory sand canvas (`#FBF9F5`) in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) bound by precise 1px hairline borders in `#E8E4DC`.
- **Subtle Ambient Lift:** Interactive states, floating map markers, and sticky navigations use a delicate, tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.08)`. The botanical emerald tint ensures elevations stay natural and cohesive.
- **Overlays & Modals:** Light, translucent scrims tinted with deep botanical green at low opacity (`rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.45)`) paired with delicate backdrop blur (`blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design adopts a soft, architectural roundedness (`roundedness: 1`):
- Standard cards, embedded media frames, and input controls utilize a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) border radius to maintain tailored, paper-like precision.
- Interactive tags, modal wrappers, and secondary card clusters step up slightly to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status markers, MRT lines, review badges, and category pill filters are intentionally fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to distinguish actionable UI tags from editorial media containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled botanical emerald green (`#0F382A`) with crisp white Plus Jakarta Sans text. Subtle hover transition shifts to `#1B2421`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Solid tropical coral (`#F26440`) reserved for booking, reservations, or high-priority discoveries.
- **Ghost / Editorial:** Outlined with 1px `#E8E4DC`, text in `#0F382A`. On hover, subtle background fill of `#F4EFE6`.

### Category Pill Filters
- Segmented pills for themes (Hawker & Dining, Heritage, Fun & Thrills, Nature, Nightlife).
- Unselected: White surface, 1px border (`#E8E4DC`), charcoal text (`#1B2421`), pill-shaped radius.
- Selected: Emerald green (`#0F382A`) surface, white text, subtle green drop shadow.

### Cards & Media
- **Discovery Card:** White surface, 1px `#E8E4DC` frame, 4px border radius. Editorial ratio image (4:3 or 16:9), Playfair Display title, muted sage meta labels, and secondary tags.
- **YouTube Embed Card:** Aspect ratio 16:9 frame with custom thumbnail overlay. Coral-filled circular play badge centered with white iconography. Creator avatar positioned bottom-left overlaid with semi-transparent frosted scrim.

### Ratings & Badges
- **Google Places Badge:** Compact inline capsule with white surface, tertiary golden spice star icon (`#F5A623`), numerical rating in bold charcoal, and total review count in muted sage.
- **MRT Transit Badges:** Authentic transit line pill capsules (e.g., Downtown Line Blue, Thomson-East Coast Brown, North-South Red) featuring standard 3-letter station codes in crisp, bold sans-serif.

### Interactive Map Markers
- Botanical green teardrop or pill marker with a crisp white center dot or iconography. Active/hovered markers scale up slightly and trigger coral accents (`#F26440`) with an ambient shadow anchor.

### Itinerary Timeline Stops
- Vertical running line in 1px `#E8E4DC` with step markers styled as 8px emerald dots. Accompanied by arrival timestamps in muted sage bold microcopy and content cards indented alongside each stop.
